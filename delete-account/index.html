Delete your MERG account

These steps apply to MERG: Carousel & Story Maker for Android (life.merg.app), published by VERENOU ENTERPRISES LLP.

Request deletion

Email care@merg.life from the Google email address you use to sign in to MERG. Use the subject “Delete my Merg account.” We may need to verify that the request came from you.

What happens to your data

  • We delete your name, email address, profile photo and user ID from Firebase Authentication within 30 days of your request.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, analytics data for up to 14 months, and support emails for up to 24 months. If you want crash or analytics data deleted, mention it in your request so we can delete what we can identify as yours.
  • Projects are stored on your device. To remove them, delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall MERG.
